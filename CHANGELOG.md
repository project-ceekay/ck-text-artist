# Changelog

## 1.0.1

- Top menu bar (`File`, `Edit`, `View`, `Style`, `Presets`, `Help`) with keyboard shortcuts and `Alt` mnemonics
- 2×-supersampled procedural icon toolbar with instant hover tooltips and action shortcuts
- Dark Mode toggle (`View -> Dark Mode` or toolbar moon icon) styling title bar, menus, toolbar, dialogs and panels
- Active font displayed in the window title bar
- Condensed 1180×760 layout with narrower left panel and compact heights for comfortable editing without vertical scrolling

## 1.0.0

First public release.

- Every installed font, with search and favourites
- Fills and outlines in a colour, gradient or image texture (with texture filters)
- Character / line spacing, squash and stretch, rotation, drop shadow or glow
- Export PNG, JPG or SVG at up to 4x quality, or drag the preview into a browser, Canva or a folder
- Presets with thumbnails, undo / redo
- Smooth window resizing: the window keeps up with the mouse while you drag its edge, and lays
  itself out once when you let go
- Settings, favourites and presets from CK Text Editor are carried over automatically
- Built-in updates: checks GitHub once a day and installs new versions in one click
